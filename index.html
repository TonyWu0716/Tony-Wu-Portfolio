ACCESSIBILITY / HEALTH TECH
APR — AUG 2024

Parkinson's Bottle Device

A bottle that reduces hand shaking and displays water temperature, connected to a mobile app that records progression of shaking patterns.

Microprocessor 3D Printing Sensors & Actuators IoT Mobile App
[ IMAGE 1 ]
Device prototype
[ IMAGE 2 ]
Mobile app — shaking pattern data
[ IMAGE 3 ]
Patient feedback & design iteration

WHY THIS PROJECT

Parkinson's patients struggle with everyday tasks, especially drinking. Existing solutions were too expensive or didn't match how people actually use them.

HOW I MADE IT

  • Bottle with a belt that tightens the patient's hand to the bottle, to reduce shaking when drinking water
  • Sticker that turns red when water temperature exceeds 40 degrees celcius, intuitively displaying water temperature
  • Connected to a mobile app that interprets hand-shaking patterns to imply disease progression
  • Sent the device to a real patient, collected feedback and iterated on the design. Total cost went down while itrations bring more functions

PROBLEM SOLVED

Safer, steadier drinking for Parkinson's patients, and a way to track shaking progression daily instead of effort-costing clinic visits.

♥
Future plans

Applying errgonimics principles in the design of the product, making it even more comfortable and easy to use
