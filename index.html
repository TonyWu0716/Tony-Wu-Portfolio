Rap Music Genre Detection

A website that classifies rap songs into sub-genres with an CNN & LSTM model — trained from a rap dataset I built from scratch, containing 8 genres.

[ IMAGE 1 ]
Dataset collection — 9 rap genres
[ IMAGE 2 ]
Confusion matrix — 90% accuracy
[ IMAGE 3 ]
Interactive website — user input

WHY THIS PROJECT

New rap listeners or producers wanting to find songs/beats they love, but don't the name of their preferred sub-genre

HOW I MADE IT

  • Dataset Engineering：Built my own new dataset of rap music spanning 8 genres & 500+ songs (older versions online are dated back to 2019)
  • AI training：Trained a model using CNN to detect tone and LSTM to detect rhythm, classify songs into sub-genres
  • Web development：Developed an interactive website for users
  • Achieved 90% accuracy from a confusion matrix test

PROBLEM SOLVED

Users can upload current songs they like, or simply play the music from the computer. The website will detect & output the sub-genre.

FUTURE PLANS

Expanding my dataset to more genres for satisfying wider needs, and including more songs to increase accuracy

ACCESSIBILITY / HEALTH TECH
APR — AUG 2024

Parkinson's Bottle Device

A bottle that reduces hand shaking and displays water temperature, connected to a mobile app that records progression of shaking patterns.

[ IMAGE 1 ]
Device prototype
[ IMAGE 2 ]
Mobile app — shaking pattern data
[ IMAGE 3 ]
Patient feedback & design iteration

WHY THIS PROJECT

Parkinson's patients struggle with everyday tasks, especially drinking. Existing solutions were too expensive or didn't match how people actually use them.

HOW I MADE IT

  • Mechanical Design：Bottle with a belt that tightens the patient's hand to the bottle, to reduce shaking when drinking water
  • Temperature Display：Sticker that turns red when water temperature exceeds 40 degrees Celsius, intuitively displaying water temperature
  • Mobile App：Connected to a mobile app that interprets hand-shaking patterns to imply disease progression
  • User Testing：Sent the device to a real patient, collected feedback and iterated on the design. Total cost went down while iterations bring more functions

PROBLEM SOLVED

Safer, steadier drinking for Parkinson's patients, and a way to track shaking progression daily instead of effort-costing clinic visits.

FUTURE PLANS

Applying ergonomics principles in the design of the product, making it even more comfortable and easy to use
